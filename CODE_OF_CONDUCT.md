# Code of Conduct

Nebula LIB is committed to a welcoming, respectful community.

Participants must:

- Be considerate and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing impact.
- Focus feedback on the work, not the person.
- Respect different experience levels and backgrounds.

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, and unwanted sexualized conduct are not welcome. Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation when needed to keep the project safe.

Report serious incidents privately to the maintainers through the contact method listed in the repository profile. Do not publish sensitive details in a public issue.
